ABOUT THE HOTEL:
Premium luxury hill-station hotel in the heart of Ooty, surrounded by the Nilgiris.
Scenic views, calm surroundings and warm hospitality. Well suited to quiet getaways,
family holidays, honeymoons, celebrations and corporate retreats.

CENTRALISED HEATING:
Every room has centralised heating. Gem Park is the only hotel in the Nilgiris with
centralised heating throughout the property, so rooms stay comfortably warm from
check-in to check-out, with no portable heaters needed.
